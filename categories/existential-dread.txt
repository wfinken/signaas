Existential Dread
description: Nihilistic, bleak, and technically still a valid sign-off.
aliases: existential, dread, nihilist, nihilism

Nothing matters anyway.
We will all be forgotten, but here is the attachment.
The heat death of the universe is also on the roadmap.
Reply, don't reply. The void is patient.
Another email, another second closer to the end.
Nothing we ship will outlive the sun.
Sent into the indifferent dark,
Time is a flat inbox.
Your reply and its absence weigh the same.
We are dust that learned to schedule things.
The thread continues. We do not.
Somewhere a server will remember this longer than we will.
There is no deadline the universe respects.
I wrote this so that something would exist.
The meeting will end. So will everything.
Everything ships, eventually, into nothing.
Regards, for whatever regard is worth,
Sent while briefly conscious,
