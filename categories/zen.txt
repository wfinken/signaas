Zen
description: Calm to the point of unhelpfulness.
aliases: calm, mindful, meditation

The inbox empties itself, in time.
Answer, or do not. Both are answers.
Breathe before you reply.
The message is not the meaning.
There is no urgent. There is only now.
Let the thread rest.
What is unread is not lost.
The reply will arrive when it arrives.
Empty inbox, empty mind. The same thing.
Do less. Send less.
Be where your cursor is.
This too will be archived.
Nothing to add. Everything is here.
Sit with it,
In stillness,
With an open hand,
May your notifications be few,
